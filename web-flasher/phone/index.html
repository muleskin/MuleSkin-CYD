Setting up. On the board: SETTINGS > PHONE ALERTS > WAITING. Then CONNECT here and pick MuleSkin-XXXX. The row says CONNECTED once this phone is listening.

Which phones. Android: Chrome. iPhone: Safari cannot do Bluetooth, so open this page in the free Bluefy browser.

Keep this page open. The browser only listens while the page is open; leaving it in the background is fine on most phones, closing the tab is not. If the board goes out of range this page keeps trying to reconnect.

Privacy. With PHONE CODE off, the first phone to connect gets the alerts. Turn it on and a phone has to send the four digits first, or the board lets it go after 30 seconds. The board takes nothing from the phone. The history and the locations are kept on this phone only (Clear deletes them); the map pictures come from OpenStreetMap.
